Robot Control Quick-Reference

1. Control Modes

open_with Free-Drive

Move joints freely. Unlocks all joints so you can manually guide the arm exactly how you want it.

sync_lock Fixed-Angle

Lock orientation. Move the arm anywhere in space, but the gripper will stay pointing in its current direction.
